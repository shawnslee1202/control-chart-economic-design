# Defensible resume bullets

- Designed and executed a 55-run economic-design experiment in a web-based manufacturing simulator, comparing subgroup allocation, control-limit width and sampling method across 825 simulated working days; estimated fixed limits from 600 pooled baseline measurements.
- Built a reproducible Python pipeline for 17,459 control-chart observations and 184,503 quality records, validating all signal counts and timestamps and applying run-level statistical comparisons and bootstrap uncertainty analysis.
- Mapped the lowest-cost sampling policy over bad-part/inspection cost ratios of 2-50; identified a modeled 7.9% base-cost reduction versus an explicit reference plan under illustrative costs, while documenting uncertainty and simulator-access limitations.

Use “guest-mode manufacturing simulator” in the project description. Do not claim a statistically significant reduction in bad parts, actual plant savings, a validated empirical detection delay, or a globally optimal policy. The 7.9% comparison is against the new n=5/h=960 reference, not the historical lab with an undocumented interval. If space is tight, use the first two bullets; they require fewer qualifications.
